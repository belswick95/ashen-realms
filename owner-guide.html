Your world. Your controls.

Open World Studio or press F8. Every change is checked by the server and recorded in Audit. The terminal accepts the game commands below; it does not run operating-system commands, JavaScript or SQL.

Build and populate

World creates permanent NPCs, monsters, buildings, walls, gates, fountains, scenery, paths, terrain and item drops. Choose a template, set its properties, then place it with a world click. Use the saved list to move, clone, delete or restore it. Export and import let you save and reuse layouts.

Actors includes original NPCs and monsters as well as your new spawns. Search by name or ID, go to one, change its stats, freeze it, respawn it, or remove it from the world. Deleted actors stay absent across clients and reconnects until restored. Objects are managed in World.

To make a shop, create a merchant NPC, set Service to Merchant / shop, and enter lines such as bait,2,1,1000: item ID, buy price, sell price, stock. Stock -1 is unlimited. Choose Banker to make an NPC open the shared bank. Keep services stationary with roam radius zero.

Items creates named trade goods and healing consumables, with prices, stackability and a choice of safe icons. Use IDs beginning custom_, such as custom_royal_bread. Items can be granted, placed, banked and stocked in shops. A healing item is consumed when clicked in inventory. New 3D equipment, uploaded meshes and arbitrary scripted abilities still require development.

Eldervale Castle

The castle is northwest of Ashen Ruins, centered at X −340, Z −305. Run castle to visit its southern approach. Cross the bridge and click the great gate to open it. Inside are the treasury, merchants, chapel, prayer altar, cooking hearth, smithy, great hall, fountain, working residents and White Knight patrols. Its 65 individual entities can be changed through World Studio.

Click the altar to restore Prayer and earn Prayer XP. Click the cooking hearth with raw fish to cook and eat it, restoring up to 12 HP and earning Cooking XP. Bankers and merchants use the same inventory, gold and shared stock systems as other settlements.

Drops, trading and town safety

Right-click an inventory item and choose Drop, then enter a quantity. Right-click the gold display to drop gold. Drops appear immediately to nearby players, who can click or right-click them to pick them up. Unclaimed drops expire after five minutes and are removed from the database.

Right-click another player and choose Trade. Both players must be nearby and out of combat. Set item quantities and gold, save your offer, then accept. Changes reset both acceptances. A final review locks the offers; both players must confirm before items and gold move atomically. Cancelled, expired or invalid trades move nothing.

The minimap stays north-up: yellow dots are NPCs and creatures, white dots are players, and red dots are ground items. Small green/red HP bars appear above combatants.

Town defenders carry longswords and scale above nearby hostile creatures. Aggressive intruders draw a guard response. Defenders are ordinary persistent NPCs in World Studio; deleting all of them removes that town’s active defenders.

The Items palette contains 35 original vector icons, including apples, water, lobster, fish, pizza, bread, meat, soup, herbs, potions, cloth, leather, bones and ore. Food categories were informed by the OSRS food list; these are original illustrations, with healing amounts you choose.

Staff and moderation

In Players, choose an existing player and set their role to Moderator or Player. Only the owner can assign or revoke roles. Moderators can kick, mute for up to 24 hours, unmute, remove chat messages and resolve reports. They cannot edit the world, grant items, assign staff roles, ban players, or moderate another staff member. Their access is checked on each command; revocation does not require signing out.

Use Server for announcements, welcome messages, maintenance and restart countdowns. A game restart saves progress and reloads clients; it does not reboot the web host or database. Audit includes before/after records and can be exported.

Design references

The control categories were informed by TrinityCore GM commands, with server permission checks following OWASP authorization guidance. The monster roster draws on familiar fantasy bestiary roles, including the OSRS bestiary; the models and castle score are original Eldervale assets.

World Studio manages the supported game systems. Hosting, database backups, code deployment and new engine features remain separate operational tasks.

Movement and the shared generated world

W/S or Up/Down move, A/D or Left/Right turn, Q/E strafe. Hold right mouse to steer with the camera; A/D strafe while it is held. Drag the scene to orbit, scroll to zoom, Space to jump and Shift to run. Touch players use the movement pad and Jump button. Portrait login is supported; play remains landscape-only.

Terrain, rivers, roads, grassland, snowy highlands and dry country use the same saved seed on every client. Birchford, Winterwatch and Emberspire replace the original towns. Eldervale Castle and owner-created objects are preserved. The previous base world is archived privately before activation. Use World studio to edit the generated town NPCs and buildings as usual; ordinary logins do not generate a new map.

Control reference: Blizzard basic movement guide. Rendering uses original procedural textures and instanced vegetation; no Warcraft or RuneScape assets are copied.
