# Combat sound credits

Every combat one-shot in this folder is cut from real recordings released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) or in the public domain. No RuneScape or other game audio is used, and nothing here is synthesised.

Processing (all files): the take was sliced out of the session recording, room tone and hiss were removed by spectral subtraction against the session's own noise floor, leading silence trimmed (3 ms pre-roll), tails gated and faded, a 45 Hz high-pass applied, loudness matched on the loudest 50 ms, peaks held under -1 dBFS, and the result encoded as mono 44.1 kHz MP3 (80 kbit/s; 96 kbit/s for roars). Some files layer two recordings or change their speed (pitch); those edits are listed per file.
