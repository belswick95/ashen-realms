# Area-sound beds

Four real field recordings from the Radio Aporee sound map, archived on the
Internet Archive. Each file is marked **Public Domain** (Creative Commons Public
Domain Mark 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/) on its
archive.org page. None of them shares a source or a file with the earlier
ambience under `assets/audio/sfx/` or `assets/audio/nature-*.mp3`.

| File | Recording | Recordist | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `day-birds.mp3` | "Rote Lache – Black Forest, morning birds", Forbach, Germany, 24 Apr 2019 | avoelzing | https://archive.org/details/aporee_44034_50101 (original `RoteLache.mp3`) | Public Domain Mark 1.0 |
| `night-crickets.mp3` | "Crickets at night near Le Derboux", Buis-les-Baronnies, France, 10 Aug 2023 | Jillis Molenaar | https://archive.org/details/aporee_70552_82256 (original `FranceMoulinDeCostLeDerbouxNightCricketsStereoBlumlein.mp3`) | Public Domain Mark 1.0 |
| `wind.mp3` | "Wind in the trees leaves [Valley Traouïero]", Trégastel, France, 22 Apr 2019 | Yponomeutaneko ! | https://archive.org/details/aporee_45007_51137 (original `CAPSValleyTraoueroWindinthetreesleaves.mp3`) | Public Domain Mark 1.0 |
| `water.mp3` | "essen, winkhauser tal, small stream at bridge", Essen, Germany, 14 May 2014 | Frank Schulte | https://archive.org/details/aporee_23391_27158 (original `EssenNaturschutzgebietBrausewindhang02.mp3`) | Public Domain Mark 1.0 |

Changes: one excerpt per recording (day 0:30–1:54, night 3:20–4:44, wind
0:20–1:45, water 0:20–1:34), high-pass filtered to remove mic rumble and mains
hum, folded to mono, the last 4–5 s crossfaded into the start for a seamless
loop (80 s; water 70 s), normalised to about −24 LUFS and encoded as 64 kb/s
mono MP3. Each excerpt was checked by spectrum and level analysis for tonal
drones, whines, voices, dogs, traffic and music before use. Nothing is
synthesized.
