# Original Eldervale music (CC BY 4.0)

Composed for Eldervale; scores in /scripts, rendered with FluidSynth and the Fluid R3 GM SoundFont (Frank Wen, MIT licence) except the NumPy-synthesized wilderness piece.

- The Gates of Eldervale: login screen, /assets/audio/login.mp3 (scripts/compose-login.py)
- Banners over Eldervale: Eldervale Castle, /assets/audio/castle.mp3 (scripts/compose-castle.py)
- Crown of the Heartlands: open wilderness, played softly, /assets/audio/wilderness.mp3 (scripts/compose-wilderness.py)
- Eldervale Level Up and Eldervale Combat Level Up: /assets/audio/level-up-skill.mp3 and level-up-combat.mp3 (scripts/build-level-jingle.py)

Wilderness environmental sounds are generated by Eldervale, not part of the composer’s music.
The fortress background is original generated Eldervale artwork. Flame, ember, mist and glow animations are original runtime effects.

# Weather sounds (CC0 1.0)

Recordings released under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) on OpenGameArt. Credited anyway, with thanks. Files live in /assets/audio/sfx/.

## AMB Rain Loop 2
- Author: Kresiek The Furry — https://opengameart.org/content/amb-rain-loop-2
- Included files: /assets/audio/sfx/amb-rain-light.mp3, /assets/audio/sfx/amb-rain-heavy.mp3
- Changes: two 30 s excerpts of the 12-minute recording (8:00 and 2:40); light bed high/low-passed (140 Hz–7 kHz, −3 dB at 3.5 kHz), heavy bed 60 Hz–11 kHz with +3 dB at 180 Hz; 4 s equal-power loop crossfade; mono, loudness-normalised (−26 / −22 LUFS), 32 kbps MP3.

## Rain + Long Thunder
- Author: WuxiaScrub — https://opengameart.org/content/rain-long-thunder
- Included file: /assets/audio/sfx/thunder-roll.mp3
- Changes: the thunder roll only (0:19.6–0:42.6), 28 Hz–6 kHz band, short fade-in and 6 s fade-out; mono, loudness-normalised (−20 LUFS), 40 kbps MP3.

## 100 CC0 SFX #2 (sfx100v2_thunder_01)
- Author: rubberduck — https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2
- Included file: /assets/audio/sfx/thunder-crack.mp3
- Changes: 30 Hz high-pass, 1.6 s fade-out; mono, loudness-normalised (−19 LUFS), 40 kbps MP3.
