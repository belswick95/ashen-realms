Professions & World Studio

Open the Skills tab and choose a non-combat skill to see its recipes, levels, tools and ingredients. Walk within four metres of a station, click it, then choose a recipe and quantity. The server handles materials, timers, experience, depletion and shared world state. Moving, changing equipment or entering combat stops production. No materials are consumed if the finished product cannot fit.

Quick start: mine → smelt → smith

  1. Buy or carry an iron pickaxe. Visit the resource trail east of Eldervale Castle. Copper rocks start near -271, -290 and tin near -266, -290. Higher ores require higher Mining levels.
  2. Mine copper and tin, then bring both to the Royal furnace at -314.6, -283.4 to smelt a bronze bar.
  3. Carry a hammer to the anvil at -310, -281. Bronze bars make nails; iron bars make the game's equippable iron armor, shields, weapons and tools. All ingredients, level requirements and outputs are visible before you start.
  4. Coal combines with iron and higher ores to produce stronger bars. Higher bars can become nails, reinforcements and metal fittings. These components can be sold, traded, or used in owner-created recipes.

Owners can run stations in World Studio → Terminal to see current station coordinates after moving anything.

Build without memorizing IDs

  1. Open World studio → Items. Search by name or category. Every item has an icon. Set the quantity and choose Add to inventory, Send to bank, or Place in world. Inventory grants never silently go to the bank; if your bag is full, choose a smaller amount or use the bank button.
  2. To place stations, choose World → New entity, select a furnace, anvil, farming patch, resource or other template, then Place with world click. Rocks have an ore selector. Trees have a log type, stock and regrowth controls.
  3. To stock a merchant, select its NPC or market entity. Add shop items using the item and price selectors. Stock -1 means unlimited; a positive number is finite stock. Save the entity.
  4. Open Recipes to edit a recipe or create one. Choose skill, station, level, XP, time, ingredients, products and tools from lists. Ingredients are consumed; tools remain. Built-in resource, fire, planting and building actions retain their special mechanics. New recipes use safe item processing.
  5. Create a custom trade good in Items. Choose an icon, a world-drop model, prices, healing amount and stackability. Then use it in recipes and shop stock. Custom bows, runes, fittings and jewelry sold as trade goods do not automatically gain new combat/equipment mechanics.
  6. Move, clone, delete and restore placed objects in World. A completed construction site has a reset button; resetting does not refund materials.

F8 opens World Studio. Moderators retain moderation tools; item grants, recipe editing and world building are owner-only and audited.

Drops & storage

Right-click an inventory item → Drop. Dropped items are visible to everyone immediately and anyone within reach can pick them up. Drops expire after five minutes and the server removes expired rows. Dropped equipment, tools, logs, ores, bars, food and supplies have representative 3D models. Custom goods can choose a drop model. Owner-placed permanent item entities remain until collected or deleted, unlike temporary player drops.

Your bank has no slot cap. It can hold multiple equipment copies. Each physical copy takes one inventory slot; noted copies share a single stack per item type. Select Withdraw as: Notes to carry them in bulk. Deposit notes and select Items to withdraw usable items. Notes cannot be equipped, eaten or used in recipes. Bait, arrows and other naturally stackable items stay stackable without notes. The inventory remains limited to 16 slots. Gold appears separately from item slots.

Bank notes follow the item/note distinction documented in the OSRS bank guide. Each note displays the original icon over a parchment background. Notes can be dropped and traded without turning into usable items.

Research and Eldervale balance

The resource-processing design is based on the documented mechanics of Mining, Smithing, Firemaking, Fletching, Herblore, Farming, Runecraft and Construction. Eldervale uses its existing level-100 curve, original items/illustrations and adjusted timings. This is a playable set of profession chains, not a recreation of every RuneScape activity, equipment tier, quest or expansion skill.
