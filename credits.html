Music files are loudness-normalized, given short endpoint fades, and compressed for the web. “Minstrel Guild” is trimmed to 3:04 with a short end fade so it loops. The compositions remain the work of Kevin MacLeod; no endorsement is implied.

“Crown of the Heartlands” was synthesized for Eldervale with lute, flute and strings; first the castle theme, it now plays quietly across the open wilderness. Its score generator is included in the project.

Level-up fanfares

“Eldervale Level Up” (every skill) and “Eldervale Combat Level Up” are original fanfares of about four seconds, composed for Eldervale and listed with the other original pieces below. No RuneScape audio, melody or arrangement is used.

The fortress gates

Original fortress artwork, animated braziers, embers, and firelight created for Eldervale.

The wilderness

Area sounds (daytime birdsong, night crickets, wind and running water) are public-domain field recordings from the Radio Aporee sound map on the Internet Archive, by avoelzing, Jillis Molenaar, Yponomeutaneko ! and Frank Schulte; sources and changes in assets/audio/ambience/CREDITS.md.

Bird calls and nighttime crickets use real CC0 backyard recordings by syncopika, with cricket source. They were filtered, normalized and faded for the game. Nearby water is mixed separately. Audio license details.

Combat sounds

Weapon swings, hits, parries, misses, bow shots, spells, grunts and monster voices are real recordings released under CC0 1.0 or in the public domain. No RuneScape or other game audio is used and none of it is synthesised. Edits: single takes sliced, background noise removed, trimmed, some layered or re-pitched, loudness-matched and encoded as small mono MP3s. Files live in assets/audio/combat/; per-file sources and edits are in assets/audio/combat/CREDITS.md.

The textured hawk and wing animation are by Wildfire Games / 0 A.D., CC BY-SA 3.0. The photo-textured butterfly and animation are by Čestmír Dammer (CDmir), with TinyWorlds and photographs by Rick Hoppmann, CC0. Models were rescaled and animations baked for browser use. Licenses and editable source files.

Rigged monsters

The animated wolf and bat are by Quaternius (Animal Pack Vol.2 and Animated Monster Pack), as are the imp (Demon), ogre (Orc), wraith (Ghost) and revenant (Ghost Skull), from his Ultimate Monsters pack; the giant rat and the troll (“Forest monster”) are by Čestmír Dammer (CDmir); the boar is by Teh_Bucket; the lava golem that plays the ash demons is by gavlig — all CC0 1.0, the Quaternius packs from Quaternius and the rest from OpenGameArt. The spider is by PantherOne and the stone golem is Dm3d’s “Rock golem”, a revision of umask007’s golem, both used under CC BY 3.0. Re-exported, trimmed and recoloured for Eldervale; sources and changes in assets/monsters/CREDITS.md.

Farms and new monsters

Cows, hens, sheep, pigs, the farm hut, chicken coop, hay bales, trough, scarecrow, flower beds, statue and wheelbarrow, plus the zombie and orc looks, are original procedural models built in code for Eldervale. No third-party assets are used.

Character animation

Animations: Adobe Mixamo. The walk and run used by players, other players and townsfolk are Mixamo motions (royalty-free for use in games), retargeted onto the Quaternius characters (CC0) and baked for the browser; the source files and build script are included in the project (assets/source/mixamo/, scripts/build-mixamo-locomotion.mjs).

Interface

Display lettering uses Cinzel by Natanael Gama, served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The stone, wood-grain, leather and parchment textures of the interface are original to Eldervale, generated in the stylesheet from SVG noise filters and CSS gradients; no third-party images are used.

Buildings

Modular building pieces (walls, roofs, doors, windows, chimneys, stairs and fences) come from the Medieval Village MegaKit by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0. Complete town buildings and castle wall pieces come from the KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack and the KayKit Medieval Builder Pack by Kay Lousberg, also released under CC0 1.0. We re-exported the pieces as compressed GLB files with webp textures; the licence texts are in assets/buildings/licenses/.

Original music: titles, authors and licences

These pieces were composed for Eldervale (melody, harmony and arrangement are original, not taken from any other game) and are released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Their scores are in the project’s scripts/ folder and were rendered with FluidSynth using the Fluid R3 General MIDI SoundFont by Frank Wen (MIT licence).
