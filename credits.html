Eldervale CastleCrown of the HeartlandsEldervale · original composition

Music files are loudness-normalized, given short endpoint fades, and compressed for the web. The login theme uses an excerpt of “Stoneworld Battle” beginning at 1:42. The compositions remain the work of Kevin MacLeod; no endorsement is implied.

The original castle composition “Crown of the Heartlands” was synthesized for Eldervale with lute, flute and strings. Its score generator is included in the project.

The fortress gates

Original fortress artwork, animated braziers, embers, and firelight created for Eldervale.

The wilderness

Wind, rustling vegetation, wildlife, and flowing water are generated environmental audio, mixed separately from the soundtrack.
