Music files are loudness-normalized, given short endpoint fades, and compressed for the web. The login theme uses an excerpt of “Stoneworld Battle” beginning at 1:42. The compositions remain the work of Kevin MacLeod; no endorsement is implied.

The original castle composition “Crown of the Heartlands” was synthesized for Eldervale with lute, flute and strings. Its score generator is included in the project.

Level-up fanfare

“Another Step in the Heartlands” is an original five-second bell and harp fanfare composed and synthesized for Eldervale. Its score generator is included in the project. No RuneScape audio is used.

The fortress gates

Original fortress artwork, animated braziers, embers, and firelight created for Eldervale.

The wilderness

Bird calls and nighttime crickets use real CC0 backyard recordings by syncopika, with cricket source. They were filtered, normalized and faded for the game. Nearby water is mixed separately. Audio license details.

Sound effects and area sounds

Real recordings, all released under CC0 1.0 (public domain) on OpenGameArt. Edits: trimmed, single hits sliced, converted to mono MP3, loudness-normalised, short fades. Files live in assets/audio/sfx/.

Farms and new monsters

Cows, hens, sheep, pigs, the farm hut, chicken coop, hay bales, trough, scarecrow, flower beds, statue and wheelbarrow, plus the zombie and orc looks, are original procedural models built in code for Eldervale. No third-party assets are used.
